Fig. 3 From a photo to brackets on the print

Read

The file, in the page

Decoded in memory. Not uploaded.

Shrink

640 px at most

A working copy. The original is let go.

Five views

Whole, then four quarters

So a small face is not shrunk away.

BlazeFace

Each view, at 128 by 128

One pass gives boxes and a score for each.

Merge

One box per face

A face seen twice is kept at its best score.

Mark

Bracket and score

Drawn over the halftone print.
